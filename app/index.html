    The road to the platinum

    Five stages, in order. Story first, strangers and activities next, the counted grinds after that, 100% completion last. The platinum pops with the last trophy you earn — usually the 100% gold.

    Possibly missable — read this first

    Five trophies may depend on a one-time window or a story choice, and the community had not confirmed them when GTA VI launched on 19 November 2026. Each one is flagged as possibly missable (unverified) — the safe play is a manual save before the moment, not a second playthrough.

    The save rule

    1. Keep one rotating manual save slot, separate from autosaves, for the whole run.
    2. Save before every partner mission, every mission that offers a choice, and before the finale.
    3. If a flagged trophy does not pop, reload the save and take the other path — do not push on and hope.

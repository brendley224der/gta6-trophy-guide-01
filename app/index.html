    Roadmap to the platinum

    Play in this order. Story first, because most trophies unlock by playing; side content next while the map is fresh; the grind last, when you own the gear and know Leonida. Nothing in stage 1 is missable — the five trophies that need care are flagged in the Missables tab.

    Missable trophies — read before you play

    Only five trophies need care. GTA VI launched 19 November 2026, so these flags are our own judgement from the official descriptions, not confirmed in-game yet: each one is tied to a moment that may not come back.
